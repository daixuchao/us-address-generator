Singapore Address Format Guide

Updated September 5, 2026

Singapore addresses often include a block or street address, an optional unit number, and a six-digit postal code. Unit numbers such as #12-05 are common in residential and office addresses.

Common layout

Tan Wei Jie
10 Anson Road
#12-05 International Plaza
Singapore 079903

Singapore government address guidance uses a house number and street name, an optional unit number, Singapore, and the postal code. Domestic forms generally do not need a state or province field.

Form testing checklist

  • Accept unit numbers such as #08-231 without stripping the hash or hyphen.
  • Preserve leading zeroes in six-digit postal codes by storing them as text.
  • Support both block-based residential and building-based commercial addresses.
  • Do not require a state, province, or region for a domestic Singapore address.
  • Test building names, mobile wrapping, printed labels, and CSV exports.
