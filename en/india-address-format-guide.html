India Address Format Guide

Updated September 5, 2026

Indian addresses can include building names, flat numbers, street names, locality, city, state, and a six-digit PIN code. Forms should allow more address lines than a simple street/city/postcode model.

Common layout

Aarav Sharma
Flat 402, Green Heights
MG Road, Indiranagar
Bengaluru, Karnataka 560038
India

India Post recommends a complete address with the house or building, street or road, locality, post town, PIN code, and state where needed. Urban and rural addresses can differ, so a form should not assume that every address follows one fixed street-and-city pattern.

Form testing checklist

  • Support at least two address lines for building, street, locality, and landmark details.
  • Keep the six-digit PIN code separate from phone-number or account-number validation.
  • Test long locality, district, state, and union-territory names without truncation.
  • Allow urban, rural, apartment, company, and post-office delivery variations.
  • Verify that line breaks and local spelling survive checkout, labels, and CSV export.
